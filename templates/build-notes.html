BUILD NOTES

How this
site works.

The room is based on photos of my real setup. I wanted the website to feel like a place that’s mine, with my projects, music, and games in it.

The room.

The illustration has links and buttons placed over the objects. The desk opens my projects, the monitor opens TriasWear, the instruments let you play a few notes, and the TV opens the arcade. The monitor uses my original TriasWear logo.

The room moves slightly with the pointer. That movement switches off on touch devices and when reduced motion is enabled.

Music and the arcade.

My tracks includes music I made, plus After Hours, which I made with Easton. The waveform follows the audio while it plays.

The guitar and piano sounds are generated in the browser. They’re playable instruments, rather than recordings of me performing.

Signal Break is a brick-breaking game. You can play with the keyboard or pointer controls, and the best score saves in the current browser when storage is available. The soundtracks, Swinging Pants and Retro Beat, are music I made.

Checks.

The checks cover page links, game behavior, and stopping audio when an instrument closes. Browser checks also cover navigation and phone-sized layouts. Physical-phone checks are a separate step.

Credits.

The direction, projects, and personal stories are mine. I also wrote and edited code for this site, with AI helping along the way.

The room illustration was also made with AI, based on photos of my real setup.
